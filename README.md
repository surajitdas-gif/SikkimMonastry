# Sikkim Monastery Project

## Overview

This project presents information about the famous monasteries of Sikkim, highlighting their cultural, historical, and religious importance.
It aims to explore Sikkim’s Buddhist heritage and promote awareness of important monasteries among students and visitors.

## Objectives

* Study important monasteries of Sikkim
* Understand their historical significance
* Explore architectural features
* Present tourism importance of monasteries
* Compare different monasteries across regions

## Major Monasteries Included

* Rumtek Monastery
* Pemayangtse Monastery
* Tashiding Monastery
* Enchey Monastery
* Phodong Monastery
