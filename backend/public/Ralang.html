Ralang Monastery

A significant Buddhist monastery of the Kagyu sect, located near Ravangla, Sikkim.

Ralang Monastery, also known as Ralong Monastery, is situated near Ravangla in South Sikkim. Founded in 1995, it belongs to the Kagyu sect of Tibetan Buddhism and has become an important spiritual center. The monastery is known for its serene environment, colorful architecture, and panoramic views of the surrounding hills.

Key Features

  • Modern Kagyu sect monastery, established in 1995.
  • Beautiful Tibetan-style architecture with bright murals and prayer halls.
  • Hosts annual religious festivals including Cham dances.
  • Houses sacred Buddhist relics and statues of revered lamas.
  • Located on a hill with scenic views of the surrounding mountains and valleys.

History & Significance

Ralang Monastery was founded to promote Buddhist teachings and provide a spiritual hub for the Kagyu sect in Sikkim. It attracts monks, devotees, and tourists alike for meditation, religious festivals, and cultural experiences.

Tourist & Travel Information

The monastery is accessible from Ravangla by road. Visitors can explore the monastery, attend prayer ceremonies, enjoy panoramic views, and participate in local festivals. Accommodation and transport options are available nearby.
