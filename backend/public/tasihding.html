Tashiding Monastery

One of the most sacred and ancient monasteries of Sikkim, located in the West District.

Tashiding Monastery, established in the 17th century, is a revered Nyingma Buddhist monastery perched on a hill near the confluence of the Rathong and Dikchu rivers. It is considered one of the holiest monasteries in Sikkim, and is famous for its spiritual sanctity, ancient relics, and the annual Bumchu festival.

Tourist & Travel Information

Tashiding is accessible from Gyalshing or Pelling via road. Visitors can explore the monastery complex, attend religious ceremonies, enjoy scenic views of rivers and hills, and experience Sikkimese cultural festivals. It is a must-visit for spiritual seekers and tourists alike.
